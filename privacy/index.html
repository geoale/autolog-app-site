Privacy Policy

AutoLog respects your privacy.

The app stores your vehicle and expense data locally on your device. If you use backup or export features, you choose where files are saved (for example, iCloud Drive or other providers via the iOS share sheet).

Subscriptions are processed by Apple via the App Store. We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status (entitlements) in the app.

The app may send local notifications for maintenance reminders. Notifications can be disabled at any time in your device settings.

The app does not require account registration or login.

We do not sell, share, or transfer your personal data to third parties.
