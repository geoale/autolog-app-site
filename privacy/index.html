Privacy Policy

AutoLog respects your privacy and is designed to work without collecting personal information.

AutoLog stores vehicle, maintenance, and expense data locally on your device. This data is not transmitted to AutoLog servers.

If you choose to use backup or export features, you decide where your data is saved (for example, iCloud Drive or other storage providers available through the iOS share sheet). AutoLog does not have access to these files once exported.

AutoLog does not require account registration or user login.

Subscriptions are processed securely by Apple through the App Store. AutoLog uses RevenueCat solely to manage subscription status (entitlements) within the app. This process may involve the use of an anonymous app-specific identifier and purchase history for the purpose of enabling subscription features. This information is not linked to your identity and is not used for tracking.

AutoLog does not use analytics services, advertising services, or tracking technologies, and does not display ads.

AutoLog may send local notifications for maintenance reminders. Notification permissions can be changed or disabled at any time in your device settings.

AutoLog does not sell, share, or transfer personal data to third parties.
