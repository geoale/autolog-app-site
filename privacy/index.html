Privacy Policy

AutoLog respects your privacy.

The app stores your vehicle and expense data locally on your device. If you use backup/export features, you choose where files are saved (e.g., iCloud Drive or other providers via the iOS share sheet).

Subscriptions are processed by Apple via the App Store. We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status (entitlement) in the app.

We do not sell your personal data.
